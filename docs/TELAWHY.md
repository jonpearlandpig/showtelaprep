# TELAwhy

TELAwhy is the trust engine. Every answer, brief, search result, registry summary, and operational recommendation must expose why it is believed.

## Required Fields

- source artifacts
- extracted evidence
- related risks
- related decisions
- related assumptions
- related entities
- reasoning summary
- confidence
- last updated

## Answer Rule

If TELAwhy cannot point to FilmAKB evidence, the answer is not allowed.

Fallback answer:

`Not found in current FilmAKB.`

## Confidence

Confidence reflects evidence coverage, recency, relationship strength, and whether assumptions remain unverified. Confidence is not model certainty.
